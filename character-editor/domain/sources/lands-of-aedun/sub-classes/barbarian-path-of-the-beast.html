Barbarian: Path of the Beast

In the Ambersea, warriors who display a ferocious battle frenzy in combat may be initiated into an ancient, primal tradition called the Path of the Beast. Initiates of this path, when entering a battle rage, physically transform into a frightening animal-human hybrid form. Wolf-human, bear-human, boar-human and other hybrid forms have all been reported. The fighter is initiated into this path by an older Path of the Beast warrior. The initiate’s animal-human hybrid form will be the same as the form of the initiating warrior. The Bear and Wolf tribes of the Ambersea each have a long history with the Path of the Beast and have a cadre of elite warriors who all assume a similar bear-human or wolf-human form respectively.

Beast form

Starting when you enter this path at 3rd level, you physically transform to an animal-hybrid form when entering a rage. When entering this form you gain the following benefits:

  • 1d12 + Constitution modifier of temporary hit points which lasts while raging.
  • +1 bonus to your armor class.
  • As a bonus action, make an unarmed bite attack for 1d6 + Strength and Rage modifiers piercing damage.

Beast sense

Beginning at 6th level, you gain the following additional benefits when in beast form:

  • You have advantage on all Wisdom checks.
  • You gain darkvision. You can see in dim light within 60 feet as if it were bright daylight and in darkness as if it were dim light.

Beast agility

Beginning at 10th level you gain the following additional benefits when in beast form:

  • Movement through non-magical difficult terrain costs you no extra movement.
  • You can move stealthily at a normal pace.
  • Opponents have disadvantage on opportunity attacks.
  • You can take the Dash action as a bonus action.

Blood sense

Beginning at 14th level you gain the following additional benefits when in beast form:

  • You are aware of the location of creatures within 30 feet of you that you cannot see and attacking such a creature does not impose disadvantage.
  • After taking damage from a creature within 5 feet of you, you may use your reaction to make a bite attack.
