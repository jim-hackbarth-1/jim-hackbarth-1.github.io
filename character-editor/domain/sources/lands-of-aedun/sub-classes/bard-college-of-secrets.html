College of Secrets

The college of secrets is a clandestine organization rumored to be headquartered in the city of Skye in the Guarded Forest. The organization is made up of traveling bards, actors, and entertainers who lead a secret double life as political agents. They are active in helping non-humans to escape from Durany and in protecting the interests of the Guarded Forest from external forces of tyranny. When you join the College of Secrets, you are recruited by a handler and become part of a small cell with whom you meet from time to time in arranged, secret rendezvous.

Tradecraft

When you join the College of Secrets at 3rd level you gain the following benefits:

  • The ability to read lips
  • +5 bonus to Perception and Insight checks
  • You can create written cyphers. Others can’t decipher a coded message you create unless you teach them, or magic is used to decipher the text.

Inspiring Word

Also at 3rd level, you gain the ability to use your wit, insight, and magic to inspire confidence and competence in others. When a creature within 60 feet, that can hear and understand you, makes an attack roll, an ability check, or a damage roll, you can use your reaction to expend one of your uses of Bardic Inspirations, rolling a Bardic Inspiration die and adding the number rolled to the creature’s roll. This feature can be used after the roll but before the DM has determined whether the attack roll or ability check has succeeded.

Spy craft

Starting at 6th level you gain the following additional abilities:

  • You learn 3 languages of your choice
  • +5 bonus to Deception and Performance checks
  • You can mimic the speech of another person, or the sounds made by other creatures. You must have heard the person speaking for at least one minute. A successful Insight check contested by your Deception check allows a listener to identify the deception.

Vicious riposte

Starting at 14th level, when you are targeted by a melee attack you can use your reaction to cast the Vicious Mockery cantrip targeting the attacker.
