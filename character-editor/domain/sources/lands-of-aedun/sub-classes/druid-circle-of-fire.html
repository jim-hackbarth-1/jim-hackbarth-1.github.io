Circle of Fire

The Circle of Fire is comprised of zealous defenders of the natural world who invoke the cleansing destruction of fire as a means of protecting the land from unnatural forces. Most druids of the Circle of Fire are found in the Ambersea and tend to lead a nomadic life but gather at sacred sites to celebrate the solstice with ritual ceremony and a massive bonfire.

Bonus cantrip

When you choose this circle at 2nd level, you gain the Produce flame cantrip.

Rage fire

Starting at 2nd level, when you are damaged by a creature you can see within 60 feet, you can use your reaction to cast Hellish rebuke as a 2nd level spell. Once you have used this feature, it cannot be used again until after completing a short or long rest.

Circle spells

Your elemental connection to fire grants you the ability to access certain spells. Once you gain access to a circle spell, you always have it prepared, and it doesn’t count against the number of spells you can prepare each day.
Druid Level Circle Spells
3rd Flame blade, Heat metal
5th Fireball, Protection from energy (fire)
7th Fire shield, Wall of fire
9th Conjure elemental (fire), Flame strike

Quick fire

Starting at 6th level, when you attack with a melee weapon you may attack twice instead of once and your attacks count as magical.

Fire shape

At 10th level you can expend two uses of Wild Shape at the same time to transform into a fire elemental.

Cleansing fire

At 14th level, when you damage a creature as a result of your Rage fire reaction, you regain hit points equal to the creature’s damage.
