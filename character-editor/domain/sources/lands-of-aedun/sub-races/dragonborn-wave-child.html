Dragonborn Wave Child

Ability Score Increase. Strength score increases by 1. Constitution score increases by 2.

Bond of the Sea. May breathe underwater. Swimming speed equals walking speed.

Bite. Make an attack role adding your proficiency bonus plus either Strength or Dexterity bonus. Bite damage is 1d6 piercing, plus either Strength or Dexterity bonus.

Kiss of the Deep. Upon touching a willing creature, the creature gains 2d6 hit points and the dragonborn loses hit points equal to one half of the total. Alternatively, after a successful bite attack, the creature makes a constitution save (8 + Constitution modifier + proficiency bonus). Upon a failed save, the creature takes an additional 2d6 necrotic damage and the dragonborn regains one half of the total necrotic damage. Half-damage upon a successful save. Whether touching a willing creature or attacking a foe, the effect increases to 3d6 at 6th level, 4d6 at 11th level, and 5d6 at 16th level. After using Kiss of the Deep, it can’t be used again until after completing a short or long rest.

Languages. Draconic and common.

Male names: Byeong-ho, Dae-jung, Do-yun, Eun-woo, Gun-woo, Ha-joon, Hwan, I-jun, Il-seong, Jae-seong, Jeong-hun, Kun-woo, Kyung, Min-ho, Moon, Pyong-ho, Seo-jin, U-jin, Woo-jin, Yeong-hwan

Female names: Bo-ra, Chae-won, Chae-yeong, Da-eun, Du-ri, Eun-ji, Eun-jeong, Gyeong-hui, Gyeong-ja, Ha-na, Hyun-joo, I-seul, Ji-an, Ji-ho, Kyung-hee, Myeong, Na-ri, Sang, Un-ju, Ye-won

During adolescence, some dragonborn develop an especially strong affinity to the sea and mature with different characteristics from most other dragonborn. These special dragonborn are known as Wave Children. The cultural standing of wave child dragonborn varies greatly in different parts of the archipelago. Some islands consider wave children to be greatly auspicious and hold them in high regard, while on other islands, wave children are thought of as untouchable and are outcast.

Wave child dragonborn are generally smaller in stature and lack a breath weapon but are often capable of great feats of stamina. They can breathe water and are equally comfortable swimming in the water as walking or running on dry land. Most notably they have an ability called the Kiss of the Deep that can transfer vitality with a touch (or bite), either healing others at personal cost or draining health from others.
