Frost Dwarf

Far to the north, in Iceland, are found the Frost Dwarves. Notable for their strength and incredible hardiness in cold weather, they are known to journey across Lake Heron as far south as Stoneport and are the only dwarves known to ever travel by boat.

Ability Score Increase. Strength score increases by 1.

Dwarven Survival. +5 bonus to Medicine and Survival checks.

Languages. Ruic (dwarven), Common
