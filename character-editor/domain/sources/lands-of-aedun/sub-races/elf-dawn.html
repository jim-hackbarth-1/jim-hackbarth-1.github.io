Dawn Elf

Far to the east, amongst the grass and steppe lands of the deep Ambersea live a subrace of elves known as the dawn elves. Like their brethren in the forests of the west, the dawn elves are a long-lived race that have a sacred affinity with the natural world. But where other elves are aloof and reserved, the dawn elves are a warm and gregarious people. They delight in storytelling and in feats of skill involving athletics and horsemanship and while still slender by human standards, they are more sturdily built than other subraces of elves. They are seldom found west of the Seareach river for they have a deep and abiding love of the open grasslands and steppe of the Ambersea.and are known to be great travelers, returning to the western Ambersea with exotic tales of the far east.

Ability Score Increase. Strength score increases by 1.

Dawn Elf Weapon Training. Proficiency with the war pick, scimitar, longbow, and shortbow.

Animal Kinship. +5 to Animal Handling skill checks.

Dawn Elf Horsemanship. Mount or dismount with no movement cost and gain advantage on any effect that might cause the rider to be dismounted.

Languages. Wullah (dawn elvish), Common
